# Habits learned (Claude as the student)

Learned from 15 lessons with feedback (golden answers, token F1), three blocks of five, a journal and
one or two changes per block. Work out every answer: evidence → reasoning → answer.

1. Answer in full sentences that restate the question's subject, in the passage's own words (not a fragment).
2. Keep the detail the passage puts right next to the fact — who said it, why, how much, the reason for each item —
   from the same or the next sentence; not further consequences, and not your own inferences.
3. Name two or three of the passage's examples, and the person or agent it credits, when the question invites them — not whole lists.
4. One sentence per part of the question; two or three sentences in all; nothing the question did not ask.
